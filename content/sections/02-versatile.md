The Polymod Framework and Operating System (Polymod FOS) is a support system for making progress under uncertain conditions.

*It is useful for anyone who has a vision and wants to realize it, but doesn't know how yet.*

It acts as a guidance system for those who are operating with limited resources in a fast-changing environment.

Establishing a strong action-reflection feedback loop is a proven technique for sense-making in a volatile and uncertain environment. The Polymod Framework and Operating System helps you mine your own logs and generate insights so that you can adapt and course-correct.

It helps you track, communicate, and integrate the hard work you pour in and the value you create for your target audience at a granular level.
