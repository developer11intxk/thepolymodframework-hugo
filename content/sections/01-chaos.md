> Globally, 90% of Startups fail. – [StartUp Statistics 2025, DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/startup-statistics/)

Solo, Early-Stage and Young Entrepreneurs (SEYEs) are independent visionaries building nascent ventures. They are driven by autonomy and passion, but they also face immense, singular challenges.

SEYEs often manage every facet of their business, leading to excessive hours, chronic procrastination, and difficulty prioritizing. Many launch without robust plans, then struggle with chaotic, inefficient operations as diverse tasks pile up.
